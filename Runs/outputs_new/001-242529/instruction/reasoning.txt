**Analyzing crowd control measures**

I need to consider the compliance of authorities while addressing public safety, crime prevention, and protection of others' rights. There's a pressing social need here since some participants had dangerous items, creating a risk of confrontation. Targeted searches to remove those items might be justified, but a blanket approach—arresting everyone and searching without individualized suspicion—seems excessive, especially when the event was peaceful. The police presence was substantial, indicating they could have managed the situation differently.
