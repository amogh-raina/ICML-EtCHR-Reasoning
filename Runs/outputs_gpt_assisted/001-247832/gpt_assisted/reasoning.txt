**Analyzing consent requirements**

I'm considering whether requiring express consent for sharing edited images is too strict. The domestic court seems to think that using an image without consent is automatically unlawful. However, Article 79(2) allows for exceptions in public contexts. It looks like the Supreme Court is reading this too narrowly by requiring consent for images that could be damaging. I wonder if Strasbourg will find that the courts didn't adequately consider public interest and circumstances, which could go against Article 10.
