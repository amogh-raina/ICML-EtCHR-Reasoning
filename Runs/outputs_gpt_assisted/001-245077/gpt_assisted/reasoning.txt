**Evaluating lawyers' roles in public debate**

I'm considering the European Court of Human Rights (ECtHR) and how it emphasizes the responsibility of lawyers in public debate. It seems that they have a right—and maybe a duty—to highlight issues related to justice, particularly concerning ill-treatment. This makes me reflect on how important it is for legal professionals to draw attention to these critical matters for the sake of public awareness and justice. It’s fascinating how those roles interconnect!
