4. **Status of the claimant**: T.A. was the chief executive of a state-owned company, appointed by presidential decree. This makes him a public figure. ECtHR has found violations when damages are disproportionate and not based on individual circumstances (see 20755/08).

8. **Retraction order**: This is generally acceptable under Article 10(2) as a sanction, but when combined with heavy damages, it may become problematic.
